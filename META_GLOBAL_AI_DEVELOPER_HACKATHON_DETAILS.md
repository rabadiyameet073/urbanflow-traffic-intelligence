## 1. Executive Summary

The **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon** is a 10-day virtual hackathon organized by **Meta Platforms, Inc.** and **Cerebral Valley Enterprises, Inc.**. The event unites global developers to build next-generation artificial intelligence applications powered by Meta's latest proprietary and open models. 

Participants gain early developer access to cutting-edge models (including the **Muse Spark** series, **Muse Wonder**, **Muse Image**, **SAM 3.1**, and the **Muse Code** coding agent) alongside developer credits provided directly by Meta and specialized inference platforms (**Baseten**, **Modal**, and **Fireworks**).

Developers compete across two primary tracks (**Indie Developer Track** and **Startup Track**) for a share of **$1,000,000 in cash prizes**, culminating in live finals and a global awards broadcast.

## 3. Tracks & Prize Money Breakdown ($1,000,000 Total)

The total cash prize pool is **$1,000,000 USD**, funded by Meta and distributed across two main competitive tracks plus community bonus awards. Every submitted project must be entered into exactly **one** track.

```
Total Prize Pool: $1,000,000 USD
├── Indie Developer Track : $400,000 USD
├── Startup Track         : $550,000 USD
└── Bonus Awards          :  $50,000 USD
```

### 1. Indie Developer Track ($400,000 USD)
* **Target Audience:** Individual builders, open-source contributors, independent software creators, and students.
* **Team Size:** Solo or teams of up to 4 members.
* **Evaluation Focus:** Creativity, technical ambition, uniqueness, and proof of functionality.
* **Prize Allocation:**
  * **1st Place:** $150,000 USD
  * **2nd Place:** $100,000 USD
  * **3rd Place:** $75,000 USD
  * **4th Place:** $50,000 USD
  * **5th Place:** $25,000 USD

### 2. Startup Track ($550,000 USD)
* **Target Audience:** Technical founders and early-stage startup teams building production-grade products.
* **Requirement:** Startup representative must be an active employee/founder authorized to act on behalf of the company.
* **Evaluation Focus:** Technical execution, production readiness, commercial viability, and product durability.
* **Prize Allocation:**
  * **1st Place:** $250,000 USD
  * **2nd Place:** $150,000 USD
  * **3rd Place:** $75,000 USD
  * **4th Place:** $50,000 USD
  * **5th Place:** $25,000 USD

### 3. Standout & Bonus Awards ($50,000 USD Aggregate)
Organizers may award up to $50,000 in bonus prizes to recognize achievements that distinguish themselves across the event. Categories include:
* **Community Vote Award** (selected through public polling)
* **Most Viral Project Award**
* **Notable / Frontier Innovation Categories** designated by the judges.
* *Note:* Bonus awards do not deduct from or reduce the track prizes.

## 4. Project Themes & Focus Areas

Submissions in either track must align with at least one of the two core hackathon themes:

### Theme 1: "Build for Your World"
* **Concept:** Bring Meta's frontier models into domains you experience every day.
* **Objective:** Solve an immediate, firsthand problem that frees up human time, reduces cognitive overhead, and makes workflows or creative experiences possible that could not exist previously.

### Theme 2: "Push the Frontier"
* **Concept:** Apply Meta's models to technical, scientific, physical, or hardware frontiers.
* **Objective:** Build solutions in robotics, industrial automation, scientific discovery, energy, novel materials, mechanical control, simulation, or software engineering infrastructure. Demonstrate how advanced AI operates when integrated into complex physical or real-world systems.

## 6. Eligibility & Participation Rules

### General Eligibility
* **Age Requirement:** Must be at least 18 years old and the age of legal majority in the participant's jurisdiction.
* **Capacity Cap:** Maximum 20,000 accepted developers worldwide.
* **Location & Sanctions Restrictions:** Void where prohibited by law. Ineligible jurisdictions and sanctioned regions include:
  * Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine (specifically the Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk regions), Syria, Cuba, North Korea, and Iran.
  * Any individuals/entities targeted by U.S., EU, UK, or UN trade sanctions or export control restrictions.
* **Disqualified Persons:** Employees, contractors, and interns of Meta Platforms, Inc., Cerebral Valley Enterprises, Inc., government-affiliated personnel, and immediate family members (spouses, partners, parents, guardians, children, siblings) residing in the same household.

### Team Structure & Representation
* **Track Exclusivity:** Participants may apply for only 1 track and submit only 1 project.
* **Team Limit:** Up to 4 members for the Indie Track.
* **Designated Representative:** Each team or startup must appoint a single "Representative" who:
  * Submits the project on behalf of the team.
  * Warrants legal authorization and confirms all members have agreed to the Official Rules and Waiver.
  * Serves as the point of contact for prize payment allocations.

## 7. Application Process & Evaluation Rubric

Applications are evaluated on a rolling basis against two equally weighted pillars:

```
Application Scoring Breakdown:
├── Project Idea Clarity & Theme Fit (50%)
└── Demonstrated Technical Skill    (50%)
```

1. **Your Project Idea (50%):** Feasibility, clarity of problem statement, and how effectively it targets "Build for Your World" or "Push the Frontier".
2. **Your Technical Skill (50%):** Technical rigor demonstrated in recent AI projects (algorithms, architectures, deployments). Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, or live shipped products are verified.

### Application Form Questions
Applicants complete the following questions upon applying:
1. *Where do you live (City, State, Country)?*
2. *What do you want to build for this hackathon?*
3. *Are you applying for the startup track or indie dev track? (Track can be adjusted later)*
4. *What country is your company incorporated in?* (Required for startup track)
5. *Tell us about an AI project you recently built that showcases your technical skills (Include what you built and the techniques used).*
6. *Have you used Meta Model API before?*
7. *How likely are you to build with Meta Model API?*
8. *How would you describe your overall opinion of Meta Model API?*
9. *How did you hear about this hackathon?*
10. *Meta Communications Consent & Data Policy confirmation.*

## 8. Submission Guidelines & Project Judging Criteria

All final projects must be submitted to the official portal:  
🔗 **[Submit Project](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit)**

### Official Hackathon Judging Rubric
All submitted hackathon projects are evaluated by a selected panel of judges using the four-point criteria below:

```
Project Judging Rubric:
├── Impact                      : 30%
├── Use of Meta AI Tooling       : 25%
├── Technical Depth & Execution : 20%
└── Working Demo & Video Quality : 25%
```

| Criterion | Weight | Evaluation Focus |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Impact** | **30%** | Real-world problem relevance, potential user base, magnitude of value created, and fit with problem statements. |
| **Use of Meta AI Tooling** | **25%** | Depth of model integration (going beyond generic API calls), creativity, and surfacing non-obvious model capabilities. |
| **Technical Depth & Execution**| **20%** | Architecture craftsmanship, code quality, stability, thoughtful engineering, and production viability. |
| **Demo Quality** | **25%** | A functional, live working demo, robustness under live testing, and the clarity/polish of the video demonstration. |

## 9. Prize Payouts, Taxes & Banking Process

The financial distribution protocol is governed by Cerebral Valley under agreement with Meta:
1. **Disbursement Administrator:** Cerebral Valley administers winner verification, tax filings, and bank transfers on behalf of Meta.
2. **Payout Provider:** Powered by **Tremendous**. Winners provide banking information directly through Tremendous’s secure gateway. Cerebral Valley never stores bank account numbers.
3. **Currency:** Paid in **USD** via direct bank wire.
4. **Mandatory Documentation:**
   * **U.S. Residents:** Form **W-9**.
   * **International / Non-U.S. Residents:** Form **W-8BEN**.
   * Signed Event Waiver and Official Rules acceptance.
5. **Team Allocations:** Teams can choose a single payout to the Representative or specify exact split percentages among teammates in writing prior to payout.
6. **Payment Window:** Completed within **30 days** after winner verification, documentation submission, and release of prize funds from Meta.
7. **Taxes & Wire Fees:** Winners are responsible for local taxes, withholding, wiring fees, and currency exchange conversions.
